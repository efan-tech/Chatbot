=== BRIGHT SMILE DENTAL CLINIC - KNOWLEDGE BASE ===

Clinic Overview:
Bright Smile Dental Clinic provides general, cosmetic, and emergency dental care services. 

Operating Hours:
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
- Sunday & Public Holidays: Closed (Emergency on-call available)

Services & Pricing:
- Dental Checkup & Consultation: KES 1,500
- Professional Teeth Cleaning & Scaling: KES 3,500
- Tooth Extraction (Simple): KES 2,500
- Surgical Tooth Extraction / Wisdom Tooth: KES 7,000 – KES 12,000
- Composite Tooth Filling: KES 3,000 per tooth
- Professional Teeth Whitening: KES 15,000
- Root Canal Treatment: KES 15,000 – KES 25,000 (depending on tooth placement)

Insurance & Payments:
- Accepted Payment Methods: M-Pesa, Cash, Visa, and Mastercard.
- Accepted Insurance Providers: NHIF/SHA, Jubilee Health, AAR, APA, and Britam.

Appointment & Cancellation Policy:
- Walk-ins are accepted, but scheduled appointments take priority.
- Patients must cancel or reschedule appointments at least 24 hours in advance.
- Emergency walk-ins are treated immediately based on triage severity.
